About Me

A quick introduction to who I am, what I build, and what I'm working toward.

I'm an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at the University at Albany who likes building systems that actually get used — an embedded monitoring system running in an active research lab, a tournament platform used by real community organizations, a hand-tracking pipeline built solo under a 24-hour clock. I care about understanding how things work end to end, not just getting a demo working.

Outside of engineering, I've spent years competing in chess, picked up the guitar after moving from India to the U.S., and keep showing up for community volunteer work — the library, the Red Cross, environmental initiatives. I'm currently an Officer with the IEEE Student Branch at UAlbany, helping shape how the branch runs events and reaches new members.
